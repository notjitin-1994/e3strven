# E3 Strategic Ventures - Website Content & Strategy Plan

## 1. Project Overview & Requirements
- **Goal**: Create a quick, simple, and static hub website for E3 Strategic Ventures.
- **Performance & Compatibility**: Must be highly responsive and lightweight. Specifically requested to avoid heavy animations ("moving websites") and high-resolution bottlenecks to ensure it loads perfectly across all devices (phones and desktops) without any credibility-damaging issues.
- **Future Scope**: Complex features, apps, and detailed inner pages will be developed later. For now, just a clean landing page.
- **Assets Expected**: Logos for E3 Strategic Ventures, E3 Media, Riyas Hakkim, and Human Sub will be provided by the client later.

## 2. Website Architecture
The website will act as a parent hub linking to various subsidiary brands and services.
- **Hero Section**: Brief introduction to E3 Strategic Ventures (Location: A1 Workspace, The Workbook, Tower 1, Hilite Business Park, Calicut).
- **About Us**: A management consulting firm dedicated to empowering organizations and professionals through leadership development, strategic consulting, and emotional sales transformation.
- **Group Companies & Services (Cards/Links)**:
  - **E3 Global / Consulting**: Links to E3 Global website. Services include Training & Coaching (Vision Workshop, Leadership & Sales Accelerators, Emotional Selling, Life Accelerator) and Consulting (Brand Amplifier, Market Feasibility, HR, Business Standardisation).
  - **E3 Media**: Links to E3 Media Events website. Handles social media, digital marketing, video production, and branding.
  - **Riyas Hakkim**: Links to Riyas Hakkim's website. Showcasing India's First Emotional Sales Leadership Coach.
  - **Human Sub**: Placeholder for future link (logo to be provided).
- **Contact Section**: Phone (+91 79070 89069), Email (Info@e3strategicventures.com), and Address.

## 4. Technical Stack Recommendation
- HTML5, Semantic Structure.
- Vanilla CSS (for ultra-fast loading and cross-device compatibility, adhering to the strict "no heavy animation / high compatibility" requirement).
- Optimized, compressed web-safe images (to address the client's past issues with unoptimized images failing to load on certain phones).
